STHIRA: AI-powered adaptive housing

A location-adaptive platform that converts a map pin into a site and risk profile, combines it with household requirements and budget, and produces explainable housing recommendations with cost estimates and 3D visualization.

Features

Each capability of the proposed platform, and where it appears in the demo below.

In demo

1. Site and risk profile

A location is converted into hazard scores for flood, landslide, cyclone, drought, earthquake and heat. The demo uses sample location types in place of a live map pin.

In demo

2. Household inputs

Plot dimensions, household size, accessibility needs and budget shape the design.

In demo

3. Explainable recommendations

Each measure for configuration, materials and mitigation is listed with the risk that triggered it.

In demo

4. Alternative concepts

Three concepts at different levels of protection, compared on cost, budget fit and estimated risk reduction.

In demo

5. Floor plan

A schematic ground-floor layout sized to the household and plot.

In demo

6. 3D visualization

An interactive three.js model that changes with the selected concept: plinth, roof form, walls, tank, ramp and more.

Planned

7. Live data integration

Terrain, climate and disaster-history data from IMD, ISRO, NDMA and state authorities.

Planned

8. Professional validation

Recommendations to be reviewed by qualified architects and structural engineers.
